The problem

  • Filing a complaint takes forms, visits and typing
  • Proof such as photos, place and time is often missing
  • Mails to authorities are written and sent by hand
  • Rallies, protests and events do not reach people
  • Help and updates are scattered across many places
Citizen × Authority

Our solution: the Samadhyan site

  • One website for complaints and civic participation
  • Report an issue with photos, text or voice
  • AI writes the mail and sends it to the right authority
  • Join rallies, protests and events
  • AI bot answers your questions
